# Playbook: AI Automation Agency

Sell implemented automations (not software) to businesses drowning in repetitive
work. You keep the tools; they buy outcomes.

## Best niche examples
- Clinics/dentists: appointment booking, reminders, no-show reduction.
- Real-estate agencies: lead qualification and follow-up sequences.
- Accounting firms: document intake, data extraction, client chasing.
- Ecommerce operators: support-ticket triage, order-status automation.
- Recruiters: candidate screening summaries and scheduling.

## Ideal customer profile
Owner-operated business, 5–50 staff, ≥1 employee doing ≥2 h/day of repetitive
digital work, already pays for software (proves willingness), owner reachable
directly. Avoid: enterprises (procurement kills you) and pre-revenue startups.

## MVP
One automation for one niche, delivered manually-behind-the-scenes if needed:
e.g. "WhatsApp booking + reminder flow for dermatology clinics, live in 7 days."
No custom platform, no dashboard — a working flow plus a weekly results email.

## Pricing model
Setup fee ₹15–40k / $200–500 + retainer ₹8–25k / $100–300 per month per
automation. Anchor against the salary cost of the hours saved (price at 30–50% of
it). Never hourly — you're selling outcomes, and your delivery time will shrink.

## Sales channels
Direct outreach (calls/WhatsApp/walk-ins for local niches), niche Facebook/WhatsApp
owner groups, referrals from the first 3 clients (ask at day 30), partnerships with
the niche's existing vendors (POS resellers, clinic-software dealers).

## Marketing channels
Before/after case studies (numbers, screenshots), a 60-second demo video per niche,
LinkedIn/Instagram posts showing the automation running. Content compounds later;
outreach pays now — 80/20 outreach for the first 90 days.

## Tech stack
Zapier/Make/n8n, WhatsApp Business API (start the approval day 1 — takes 1–3
weeks), Google Sheets/Airtable as the client-visible database, Claude API for
classification/drafting steps, Tally/Typeform for intake. Custom code only where a
connector is missing.

## First 10 customers strategy
List 100 businesses in ONE niche in ONE city → contact 20/week with a
specific-to-them observation ("you replied to my booking message in 9 hours") →
offer a 2-week free pilot capped at 3 slots → convert pilots at the promised price;
ask each for 2 referrals. Expect: 100 contacted → ~15 conversations → ~5 pilots →
3–5 paying.

## 30-day launch plan
- **Week 1:** pick niche; list 100 targets; file WhatsApp API application; build the
  demo flow on your own number; contact first 20.
- **Week 2:** 5+ discovery calls; sign 2–3 pilots; deliver pilot #1 within 72 h of
  signing.
- **Week 3:** deliver remaining pilots; send weekly results email with hard numbers
  (hours saved, bookings captured).
- **Week 4:** convert pilots to paid; write case study #1; ask for referrals; raise
  the setup fee for the next cohort.

## Common mistakes
Selling "AI" instead of an outcome; serving 5 niches at once (nothing reusable);
custom-building what Zapier does; pricing hourly; skipping the pilot→paid
conversation; scaling outreach before one automation is boringly reliable.

## Risks
Platform dependency (WhatsApp/Meta policy changes); client data privacy (get a
simple DPA signed); delivery bottleneck = you (document every flow from client #1);
automation errors harming a client's customers (add human-review steps early).

## KPIs
Outreach→conversation rate (target ≥10%), pilot→paid conversion (≥60%), MRR,
churn (<5%/mo), delivery hours per client per month (should fall), hours saved per
client (the number that sells the next client).

## Scaling path
1 niche × 1 city → same niche, more cities (playbook reuse) → hire a delivery VA at
~8 clients → productize the niche's flow into fixed packages → the package becomes a
Micro-SaaS (see `micro-saas.md`) with the agency as its distribution.
